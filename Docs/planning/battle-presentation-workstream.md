# Battle Presentation Workstream

**Status**: Draft
**Created**: 2026-04-27
**Owner**: Codex planning pass

## Objective

Turn the project from a framework-heavy prototype into a battle-presentation-first production effort focused on 3D staging, camera language, VFX timing, role animation, and strong audiovisual feedback while preserving deterministic card and turn rules.

## Workstream Split

### 1. Rule Core
- deterministic turn order
- card legality and target validation
- damage/heal/status resolution
- battle log and testability

### 2. Battle Presentation
- action staging
- Cinemachine camera states
- Timeline-driven hero moments
- VFX, hit timing, and time-scale emphasis
- damage/heal/status UI feedback

### 3. Content Data
- cards
- roles and skills
- action definitions
- camera and VFX presets
- presentation tags

### 4. AI-First Production Pipeline
- concept art and look-dev generation
- shot ideation and animation reference
- document retrieval and synthesis
- QA/report helpers
